Features

Everything HR does each month, in one place.

From the first punch-in to the bank transfer file — no spreadsheets in between.

Attendance

Daily in/out times, single or split shifts, late minutes and overtime. Lock a period once it is checked.

Leave

Your own leave types and yearly entitlements, balances per employee, paid and unpaid days, leave encashment.

Salary runs

Your pay cycle (for example 21st to 20th), allowances, normal and holiday overtime, deductions — then approve and lock.

Loans & advances

Monthly installments are deducted from salary automatically until the loan is paid off.

End of service (EOSB)

Indemnity for resignation or termination, calculated from service years and salary under Kuwait labour-law rules.

PIFSS

Employee and employer social-security contributions for Kuwaiti staff, with rates you control.

WPS bank file & payslips

Export the salary transfer file for your bank and print or PDF payslips for every employee.

Documents & expiry alerts

Civil ID, passport and contract dates with warnings before they expire, plus company document tracking.

Branches & roles

Employees by branch, and logins for your HR team with exactly the permissions you choose.

FAQ

Questions and answers.

Not answered here? Message us on WhatsApp.

Does it need the internet?
No. CTMS Payroll runs on a PC in your office and your team uses it in the browser on your network. The internet is only needed for updates.
Is it in Arabic?
Yes. Each user can switch between Arabic and English.
Does it follow Kuwait labour law?
End-of-service, PIFSS and WPS are built in, and the factors and rates are settings you can review with your accountant.
Where is our data kept?
On your own PC, with an automatic backup every hour. Nothing is stored on our servers.
Can our HR staff have their own logins?
Yes. It comes with an HR Officer role, and you can create your own roles and choose exactly what each one can see and do.
