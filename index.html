Questions

Frequently asked.

Does CTMS work without an internet connection?
Yes. CTMS runs as a Windows service on your own machine and works fully offline — the front desk keeps billing and booking even when the internet is down. Cloud hosting is available if you prefer it.
Where is our data kept, and who can see it?
Your data stays on your hardware — it is never held in someone else's cloud unless you choose hosting. CTMS makes automatic encrypted backups and verified safety snapshots, and every screen is gated by granular, role-based permissions.
Can it run more than one branch?
Yes. Add locations whenever you grow — each branch keeps its own profit & loss and stock, consolidated into a single Owner Insights view for the group.
Do you support Arabic?
Fully — a complete Arabic, right-to-left interface, along with Civil ID duplicate detection and KWD-first billing and reporting. This very page has an EN ⇄ عربية switch in the top bar.
How do we move our existing data in?
We handle migration during onboarding — patients, services, staff and stock — so you go live with your real data rather than starting from an empty system.
How much does it cost?
Pricing depends on how many branches and which modules you need. Request a demo and we'll put together a plan that fits your clinic.
