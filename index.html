Deployment

Deployed the way your clinic operates.

01

On-Premise

CTMS runs as a managed service on a server or PC inside your clinic. Staff open it from any browser on your network.

  • Keeps working when the internet is down
  • Data never leaves your premises
02

Cloud

We host and maintain CTMS on a private server for you. Access it securely from any location, with no hardware to manage.

  • Private server — not shared tenancy
  • Updates and backups managed for you
03

Hybrid / Multi-Location

Combine local installations at each site with central access for management. We design the architecture around your branches and connectivity.

  • Local speed and resilience per branch
  • Consolidated reporting for owners

Internet down? The front desk keeps working. With on-premise and hybrid deployments, CTMS continues to run locally when connectivity is unavailable — booking, billing and dispensing carry on as normal.
